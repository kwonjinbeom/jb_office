## Busan at a Glance

::: Info | Busan at a glance
📍 Where | Southeast coast of Korea. Busan (부산) is the second-largest city.
🗓 Best time | Early November: clear, cool days. Bring a light jacket for the sea wind at night.
⏱ Days needed | 2 days is enough for the classic sights. 3 is more relaxed.
🚆 From Seoul / Busan / Incheon Airport | Seoul Station to Busan Station by KTX, about 2.5 hours. From Incheon Airport take AREX to Seoul Station first. From Gimhae Airport take the light rail (Busan-Gimhae Line), then the subway.
💳 Pay | Foreign credit cards work in most shops and restaurants. Buy a T-money card at a convenience store for subway and bus.
🛂 Entry | Many passports can enter without K-ETA until 31 Dec 2026. Check your country on the official K-ETA site before you fly.
🔤 Search names | 부산역 · 감천문화마을 · 자갈치시장 · 해동용궁사 · 해운대해수욕장 · 광안리해수욕장

## Before You Go (2026 Changes)

- **KTX and SRT merged on 1 Sep 2026.** Fares fell by about 10% on average. Seoul to Busan in standard class went from ₩59,800 to ₩54,400.
- **Train tickets open 2 months before departure** from 1 Oct 2026, so 6 Nov tickets are already on sale.
- **Naver Map accepts passport verification since 4 Jun 2026.** You can book and pay in Naver Map without a Korean phone number.
- **Paid fireworks seats are on sale** (₩100,000 R seat, ₩70,000 S seat). The early-bird batch sold out in one minute.
- **The K-ETA exemption ends 31 Dec 2026.** No extension had been announced as of 27 Oct.

## Getting Around

- **Subway.** Busan Metro is simple. Line 1 goes through Busan Station, Jagalchi, Nampo and Seomyeon. Line 2 goes through Seomyeon, the Gwangalli area (Geumnyeonsan and Gwangan stations) and Haeundae. The base fare is about ₩1,600 with a T-money card.
- **Taxi.** Use Kakao T or a street taxi. Show the Korean name from your phone.
- **Bus.** Useful for Gamcheon and the temple. Use Naver Map for the live bus number.
- **Luggage.** Busan Station has lockers. Leave your bag there if your hotel check-in is late.

## Fireworks Night: Busan Fireworks Festival (부산불꽃축제)

The 21st festival is on Saturday 7 Nov 2026. The show is over the water in front of Gwangan Bridge (광안대교). The main show runs 19:00–20:00, and events start in the afternoon. Times can change with the weather, so check the festival notice the day before.

| Option | Where | Cost (checked) | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free viewing | Both sides of Gwangalli Beach and the promenade | Free | Budget travellers who come early |
| Paid R seat | Beach in front of Hotel Aqua Palace and Homers Hotel | ₩100,000 (table and chairs) | Small groups who want a table |
| Paid S seat | Same seating area | ₩70,000 (chairs only) | Couples and solo travellers |

Tickets are on NOL Ticket (World NOL has an English page) and the Busan Bank app. Busan Bank branches sell S seats only. Busan residents get 10% off. The early-bird round sold out in one minute, so check NOL Ticket to see what is left. Official source: [Busan Metropolitan City press release](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/ai-translated-press-releases/1752780).

**If you have no ticket:**
1. Arrive in the Gwangalli area by 15:00. Free spots fill from the afternoon.
2. Bring a mat, a warm layer, water and a bag for your trash.
3. Use the toilet before 18:00. The lines get long.
4. The sea wind is cold at night in November.

**Getting home.** The roads and stations near Gwangalli are very crowded right after the show. Two simple plans:
- Walk 15–20 minutes to a quieter Line 2 station before you use the subway.
- Or sit down for a late snack and leave around 21:00.

Extra subway trains and road closures were not announced when this was checked on 27 Oct. Look at the Busan Transportation Corporation notices and Naver Map nearer the date.

**Back to Seoul.** The last KTX may be too early to catch after the show. A Saturday-night stay and a Sunday-morning train is calmer.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Pay.** Cards work widely. Keep ₩20,000–30,000 in cash for small stalls. A T-money card works on the subway, on buses and in convenience stores.
- **Apps.** Naver Map (best in Korea), Kakao T (taxi), Papago (translation). The Google Maps links in this guide still open the place.
- **Tax refund.** Shops with a Tax Free sign give an instant refund if you spend over the minimum. Ask at the shop.
- **Help.** Call 1330, the Korea Travel Helpline (24 hours, English, Japanese, Chinese). From abroad: +82-2-1330.

## FAQ

**Where do I buy KTX tickets as a foreigner?**
Use the Korail English site or the Korail+ app. Enter your name as in your passport.

**Do I need a paid seat for the fireworks?**
No. The beach sides and the promenade are free, but they fill up from the afternoon. A paid seat gives you a fixed place.

**What if it rains?**
Check the festival notice and your ticket terms on NOL Ticket. Do not book a late train home the same night.

**Can I leave my bags?**
Yes. Busan Station has lockers. You can also ask your hotel to keep bags before check-in.
