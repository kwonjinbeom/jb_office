Spend Day 1 above the clouds on Mt. Seorak (설악산) and on the hand-pulled ferry at Abai Village (아바이마을). Day 2 is the beach, the fish market and the bus home. We could not confirm the cable car's November maintenance days, so call before you go.

Sokcho (속초) is a port town on Korea's east coast, with the sea on one side and the Seorak mountains on the other. In early November the leaves on the high ridges are usually gone, so this trip is about views, seafood and slow walks.

This plan is for first-time visitors who travel by bus from Seoul and have no car. Where we could not confirm a number, we say so.

## Before You Go (2026 Changes)

- **Cable car tickets are sold only on the day.** There is no advance booking on the official site. Arrive early on weekends.
- **Seoraksan entry is free** (since May 2023). Parking at the main gate is not free: one guide reports about ₩6,000 a day.
- **Osaek Cable Car (오색케이블카) is a different project.** A July 2026 report said its construction was still delayed. This plan uses the existing Seorak Cable Car (설악 케이블카), which goes up to Gwongeumseong Fortress (권금성).
- **Naver Map passport verification** (since 4 June 2026): you can use it without a Korean phone number.
- **K-ETA exemption ends 31 Dec 2026.** We did not find a 2027 extension. Check the official site before you book.

## Getting Around

**Sokcho is small, but Mt. Seorak is not in town.** The cable car station is at the Seoraksan main gate (소공원), a short ride from the city. The market and Abai Village are in the city centre, close to each other.

- **City bus** to Seoraksan: the route number and fare are not confirmed here. Open Naver Map and search "설악산 소공원" for the live arrival times. Pay with a transit card.
- **Taxi** is the backup, and Kakao T works. We could not confirm a fare, so ask the driver for the meter.
- **Abai Village and the market** are about a 10-minute walk apart. You do not need a bus.
- Seoraksan National Park Office: +82-33-801-0900.
- Cable car office: +82-33-636-4300.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Transit card.** A Korean transit card such as T-money works on Sokcho city buses. Keep cash for the ferry and market stalls.
- **Foreign cards.** The cable car and most restaurants take Visa and Mastercard. Carry ₩30,000–50,000 in cash for small stalls.
- **Naver Map.** Better than Google Maps for Korean buses and live arrival times. Since 4 June 2026 you can verify with your passport and book or pay without a Korean number.
- **Kakao T and Papago.** Kakao T for taxis, Papago for translating menus.
- **Tax refund.** Look for "Tax Free" shops. From 2024, an instant refund works up to under ₩1,000,000 per purchase and ₩5,000,000 per trip. Ask at the shop, as rules can change.
- **1330 travel hotline.** 24-hour help in English, Japanese and Chinese. Dial 1330 in Korea or +82-2-1330 from abroad.
- **Cable car office.** Ask about wind, closures and the last ride: +82-33-636-4300.

## FAQ

### Can I visit Sokcho as a day trip from Seoul?
It is possible with the 07:00 bus and the 20:30 bus home, but the trip alone is almost five hours. One night is much more comfortable.

### Do I need to book the Seoraksan cable car?
No. Tickets are sold only on the day at the ticket office. There is no advance booking. If the wind is strong, service stops and unused tickets are refunded in full.

### Are the autumn leaves still there in early November?
The Korea Forest Service predicted the Seoraksan peak for 20 Oct, so the high ridges are likely bare. We could not find a report for the lower trails. Look at the photos on Naver before you go.

### Do I need a K-ETA to visit Korea in 2027?
The exemption for 22 countries and regions ends on 31 Dec 2026 (KST). We did not find a 2027 extension. Check k-eta.go.kr before you book.

### Is the cable car closed for maintenance in November?
We could not confirm this. The official notice page did not load on 28 Oct 2026. Call +82-33-636-4300 or check the homepage on the morning of your visit.

The best single tip: buy the cable car ticket first thing, and eat only after you come down.
