# 3 Days in Kyoto (Late Nov 2026): Autumn Leaves Itinerary + Japan's New Tax-Free Refund Rules

> This plan is for first-timers who reach Kyoto in late November 2026: one area a day, with Higashiyama, then Arashiyama, then Fushimi Inari and Kurama–Kibune. From 1 November the HARUKA visitor ticket to Kyoto rises to ¥2,700, but it still costs ¥100 less than the airport bus. ## What's New in 2026

Four rules changed this year. Most English guides still show the old ones.

| Change | Before | Now | Starts |
|---|---|---|---|

### How the new tax refund works

- Spend at least ¥5,000 (before tax) in one shop on one day.
- Within 90 days of buying, show your passport at a customs kiosk at your departure airport. Do this before you check in your bags, and keep all the goods with you.
- At Kansai, Narita, Haneda, Chubu, Fukuoka, New Chitose and Naha airports, you can do this step online in Visit Japan Web, on the departure-lobby Wi-Fi.
- The shop or its refund company sends the money back. Methods vary by shop: bank, card, app or cash at the airport. Ask how when you pay.
- One missing item cancels the whole receipt. Food or cosmetics you used up in Japan get no refund.

A quick example: an item marked ¥10,000 plus 10% tax. You pay ¥11,000 at the shop. After the customs check, you can get up to ¥1,000 back, minus any fee your refund company charges.

Not yet: Kyoto plans different city-bus fares for residents and visitors (visitors ¥350–400) from fiscal year 2027. In November 2026 the bus fare is still a flat ¥230.

The shrine has no gate and no ticket. You can walk the torii tunnels at any hour (free). Go up as far as the Yotsutsuji (四ツ辻) viewpoint, then come back. That takes about 90 minutes. The full loop to the summit takes 2–3 hours, which would break this day.

### Morning: Tofuku-ji at opening time

Tofuku-ji's bridge, Tsutenkyo, is the most crowded maple view in Kyoto. The temple itself warns of heavy crowds in autumn. It is only one Keihan stop from Fushimi Inari, so you can be at the gate before it opens at 8:30. Later in the day you join the full crowd, and the last entry is 16:00.

### Getting around Kyoto

- ICOCA card: ¥2,000, including a ¥500 deposit you get back. You must pay cash for it.
- City buses: flat ¥230 a ride. The Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass is ¥1,100, so it pays off only with five or more bus rides. This plan uses trains most of the time.
- In leaf season, buses to Higashiyama and Arashiyama get very full. Take Keihan, the JR Nara Line, the JR Sagano Line or the Tozai subway instead.
- Check the City of Kyoto's [congestion forecast](https://global.kyoto.travel/en/comfort/) for your dates. It rates crowds by place and hour on a 5-step scale.

## FAQ

### Is late November too late for Kyoto's leaves in 2026?

No. This year it is the right time. Weathernews says Arashiyama will peak from about 24 November, and the city maple forecast is about 11 December. On 26 October every famous spot was still green.

### Do I need a rail pass for this plan?

No. All the trains and buses on Days 1–3 add up to about ¥3,650. A ¥1,100 bus day pass or the ¥2,100 Kurama–Kibune pass would cost you more on this route.

### I fly home from Tokyo. Can I still get my tax refund?

Yes. You get it at your departure airport, within 90 days of buying. Narita and Haneda also let you do the customs step in Visit Japan Web.

### Do I need to book Eikando's night light-up?

The official 2026 page does not mention booking. You buy a ticket at the gate between 17:30 and 20:30. Come at 17:15 to stand near the front of the queue.

The best move this week: book a room for 24 November–1 December, and buy your HARUKA ticket online before 1 November to save ¥500.
