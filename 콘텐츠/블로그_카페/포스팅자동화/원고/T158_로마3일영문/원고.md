# 3 Days in Rome (Dec 2026): Book These Three Things First

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> This plan is for first-time visitors from the US, UK and Australia who want three full days in Rome in December. Book the Colosseum and the Vatican Museums first, and pay the new €2 Trevi Fountain fee at the gate. Fares and hours checked on 26 Oct 2026.

## Trip at a Glance

::: Info | Rome in 3 days
🗓 Best for | First visit, walkers, history lovers
🌡 Weather | Cool and often wet in December. Bring a rain jacket and comfortable shoes
💱 Currency & cards | Euro (€). Cards work almost everywhere. The Trevi Fountain gate takes cards only
🛂 Entry (US/UK/AU) | No visa for short stays. See the checklist below
✈️ From the airport | Leonardo Express train, Fiumicino to Termini, €14 (secondary source, check before you go)
💸 Daily budget | About €40 to €58 (budget) or €80 to €103 (mid-range) per person, hotel not included
:::

## What's New in 2026

- **Trevi Fountain now has a fee.** Since 2 Feb 2026, you need a €2 ticket to go down to the basin, the area closest to the fountain. Hours are 9:00 to 22:00 daily. Pay online or at the gate (card only). Rome residents, children under 5 and people with disabilities enter free. (Turismo Roma, checked 27 Oct 2026)
- **EES is live.** The EU Entry/Exit System has run at all Schengen borders since 10 April 2026. On your first entry you may give fingerprints and a photo at a booth. It is free. Plan for a longer queue.
- **ETIAS is not here yet.** The EU says it will start in the last quarter of 2026 and will announce the exact date later. Do not pay a site that sells "ETIAS" before the official launch. Use travel-europe.europa.eu only.
- **Colosseum winter hours.** From 25 Oct 2026 to 28 Feb 2027 it opens 8:30 to 16:30, with last entry at 15:30. Be inside well before the early dark.

## Booking Order

Book in this order. The first one sells out the fastest.

| Order | What | Official price (Checked 26 Oct 2026) | When to book | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine (24 h ticket, timed entry) | €18 | Sales open 30 days before the date | colosseo.it |
| 2 | Vatican Museums | €20 at the door, €25 with online skip-the-line | As soon as you fix the date | museivaticani.va |
| 3 | Trevi Fountain basin | €2 | Online, or at the gate by card | turismoroma.it |

Two warnings. The Vatican Museums are closed on 8 Dec and on 25 and 26 Dec. They are also closed on Sundays, except the last Sunday of the month (free, 9:00 to 14:00, very crowded). The Colosseum is reported to close on 25 Dec and 1 Jan, but I could not confirm this on the official site, so check your dates there.

### Afternoon

Use only the official site. Look-alike sites sell the same ticket for much more. The museums are large, so take the shortest route through the Sistine Chapel and do not try to see every room.

### Afternoon

St. Peter's Basilica is next to the museums but has its own security line. Dress rules apply: covered shoulders and knees. Check opening hours on vatican.va on the day, as they change for church events. I did not verify the basilica hours.

## Entry & Money Checklist

- US, UK and Australian passport holders can visit for up to 90 days in any 180 days without a visa. Check your own government page below before you fly.
- UK passports: issued less than 10 years before arrival, and valid at least 3 months after you leave.
- EES: free fingerprints and photo on first entry.
- ETIAS: not required yet. Follow official news only on travel-europe.europa.eu.
- Cards: carry a card with no foreign fee, and some cash for small cafes.

## FAQ

**Can I buy Colosseum tickets on the day?**
Entry is by timed slot, and sales open 30 days before. Plan to book early.

**Are the Vatican Museums open on Sundays in December?**
Only on the last Sunday (27 Dec 2026): free, 9:00 to 14:00, and very crowded. They are closed on 8, 25 and 26 Dec.

**Is the Trevi Fountain free?**
You can see it from the square. The €2 ticket is for the basin area.

**How long should I allow for the Vatican?**
At least 3 hours, plus the basilica.
