# 2 Days in Chuncheon (Nov 2026): ITX-Cheongchun from Seoul, Soyang Skywalk and Nami Island

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> Fares and hours checked on 31 Oct 2026: the ITX-Cheongchun train takes about 70 to 85 minutes from Yongsan Station in Seoul to Chuncheon, and a standard ticket is reported at ₩9,800. Spend Day 1 in the city (dakgalbi, the Soyang River Skywalk and the lake path), and Day 2 on Nami Island before the train home. We could not open Korail's own fare table, so every price here is marked "reported".

Chuncheon (춘천) is a lake city in Gangwon Province, east of Seoul. It is known for spicy stir-fried chicken, called dakgalbi (닭갈비), and for its rivers and lakes.

This plan is for first-time visitors who travel by train and have no car. Where we could not confirm a number, we say so.

## Before You Go (2026 Changes)

- **The autumn Makguksu and Dakgalbi Festival (막국수닭갈비축제) near Gongjicheon Stream ended on 18 Oct 2026**, according to a YTN report of 14 Oct. In November you can still eat dakgalbi, but there is no festival.
- **The Soyang River Skywalk (소양강스카이워크) is closed every Tuesday.** In winter months (Oct to Mar) the last entry is reported as 17:30. This plan visits on a Friday.
- **Naver Map passport verification** (since 4 June 2026): you can use it without a Korean phone number.
- **K-ETA exemption ends 31 Dec 2026.** We did not find an official 2027 extension. Check the official site before you book.
- **Prices differ between websites.** For the ITX fare we found ₩6,900, ₩9,600 and ₩9,800 in different places. The first two are for other stations. We use ₩9,800 because two sources agree on it for Yongsan to Chuncheon.

## Getting There

The ITX-Cheongchun (ITX-청춘) is a Korail train on the Gyeongchun Line. It runs from Yongsan (용산) and Cheongnyangni (청량리) to Chuncheon (춘천). Some trains stop at Gapyeong (가평) and Namchuncheon (남춘천) on the way. It has reserved seats and also tickets without a seat number, so ask for a reserved seat on a busy day.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Train tickets:** use the Korail English site or app. Bring your passport to the counter. Ask for a reserved seat.
- **Maps:** Naver Map (with passport sign-up) and Kakao Map work better than Google Maps for buses and walking in Korea. Kakao T is the taxi app.
- **Translate:** Papago helps with menus.
- **Payment:** foreign cards work in most places. Keep some cash for small stalls.
- **Tax refund:** at shops that offer it, ask the shop for the current rules before you buy.
- **Help line:** 1330 is the Korea Travel Hotline, open 24 hours, with English.

## FAQ

### Can I do Chuncheon as a day trip from Seoul?

Yes, the trip takes about 75 minutes each way. But with dakgalbi, the skywalk and Nami Island, one night is more relaxed.

### Do I need a reserved seat on the ITX-Cheongchun?

The train sells reserved seats and tickets without a seat number. On Friday and weekend trains, book a reserved seat early.

### Is there still a food festival in November?

No. The Makguksu and Dakgalbi Festival ended on 18 Oct 2026. The restaurants are still open.

### How much is the entry to Nami Island for a foreigner?

We found ₩19,000 for an adult with ferry. We did not find a separate foreigner price, so check the official page.

### Will the K-ETA exemption continue in 2027?

We did not find an official 2027 extension. Check k-eta.go.kr before you book.
