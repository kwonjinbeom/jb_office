# 3 Days in London (Dec 2026): UK ETA Before You Fly, Winter Wonderland and the Contactless Daily Cap Maths

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> This plan is for first-time visitors from the US and Australia who want three days in London in December. Day 1 is Westminster, Day 2 is the Guard change, the British Museum and Winter Wonderland, and Day 3 is the Tower of London and the City. You need a UK ETA (£20) before you fly, and a contactless card on the Tube pays no more than £8.90 a day in Zones 1-2. Fares and hours checked on 31 Oct 2026, and anything I could not confirm on an official page is marked.

## What's New in 2026

- **You need a UK ETA.** Visitors from the US, Australia and many other countries usually need an Electronic Travel Authorisation instead of a visa. It costs £20 and the fee is not refunded. Apply in the official UK ETA app or on GOV.UK. You usually get an email within 1 working day, and it can take up to 3. An ETA lasts 2 years or until your passport expires, whichever is first, and covers unlimited trips (GOV.UK, checked 31 Oct 2026).
- **Use only the official service.** GOV.UK warns that other websites charge more. Do not pay a third-party "ETA service".
- **Tube single fares rose on 1 March 2026.** A Zone 1 trip is now £3.10 at peak and £3.00 off-peak. The daily caps did not rise (London.gov.uk, TfL).
- **Winter Wonderland runs 19 Nov 2026 to 3 Jan 2027.** It is closed on Christmas Day and closes at 10pm every night. Entry needs a time-slot ticket, even at off-peak times (official ticket guide).
- **Big sights are closed over Christmas.** The British Museum is shut 24 to 26 December, and one guide says the Tower of London is shut 24 to 26 December and 1 January. These dates are why this plan runs 3 to 5 December.

## Contactless Daily Cap: The Maths

TfL has one rule that matters for visitors. Pay with the same contactless card or phone for every tap, and your fares are capped for the day. If you switch cards or devices, TfL says it cannot cap them. The daily cap in Zones 1-2 is £8.90, peak or off-peak. The Monday to Sunday weekly cap is £44.70 (TfL 2026 adult fares PDF, checked 31 Oct 2026).

The prices used: a Zone 1 single fare is £3.10 at peak and £3.00 off-peak (since 1 March 2026). I used the higher peak price to be safe. A one-day Travelcard costs £16.60 and a 7-day one costs £44.70.

| Day | Tube rides | Pay each ride (peak) | With the daily cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 · Thu | 2 | £6.20 | £6.20 |
| Day 2 · Fri | 4 | £12.40 | £8.90 |
| Day 3 · Sat | 3 | £9.30 | £8.90 |
| Total | 9 | £27.90 | £24.00 |

The cap saves about £3.90 on this plan. It starts to work from the 3rd ride on a day (at peak, 3 rides cost £9.30, more than £8.90) and on Friday from the 3rd ride too, so a 4-ride day costs the same as a 3-ride day. A one-day Travelcard (£16.60) or the 7-day card (£44.70) would cost more than the whole trip on a card.

Two limits. First, caps do not cover Heathrow Express or Southeastern high-speed trains (TfL). Second, I could not confirm today's bus fare. It was frozen at £1.75 until 5 July 2026, with a £5.25 daily bus cap, but I did not find what applies now. A bus ride is a good swap for a short Tube hop in the City.

## FAQ

### Do I need an ETA to visit London from the US or Australia?

Yes, in most cases. The ETA costs £20 and lasts 2 years or until your passport expires. Apply in the UK ETA app or on GOV.UK. Use the check tool on GOV.UK to be sure for your passport.

### How long does the ETA take?

Usually one working day, and up to three. Apply well before you fly, and do not book a non-refundable trip before you have it, because the fee is not refunded.

### Is the Tube daily cap worth it?

Yes, from the third ride of a day. In Zones 1-2 you never pay more than £8.90 a day, and a one-day Travelcard at £16.60 costs almost double.

### Is Winter Wonderland free?

No. A time-slot ticket is needed. The cheapest slots are £1.00 or free at off-peak, standard slots are £5.50 and peak slots are £8.25. Rides and food are extra.

### Does the Guard change happen every day?

No. In winter it usually runs on alternate days, but dates are not fixed far ahead. Check the Household Division website for the date you plan to go.
