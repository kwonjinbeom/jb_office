# 3 Days in Prague (Dec 2026): Christmas Markets, Old Town and the Transport Ticket Maths

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> This plan is for first-time visitors from the US, UK and Australia who want three days of Christmas markets in Prague. Day 1 is the Old Town, Day 2 is the Castle and Charles Bridge, and Day 3 is Vyšehrad and a last night at the markets. You will walk most of it: five public transport rides cost 230 CZK, so the 72-hour pass (340 CZK) does not pay off. Fares and hours checked on 31 Oct 2026, and anything I could not confirm on an official page is marked.

## Trip at a Glance

::: Info | Prague in 3 days
Best for | First visit, Christmas market fans, walkers, history lovers
Dates in this plan | Fri 4 Dec to Sun 6 Dec 2026. The main markets are open 28 Nov 2026 to 6 Jan 2027
Weather | Cold, short days and often grey. Bring a warm coat, gloves, a hat and shoes with grip for wet cobblestones
Currency & cards | Czech koruna (CZK). About 21.8 CZK per US$1 (Czech National Bank, 30 Oct 2026, so check on the day). Cards work in most shops. Market stalls may prefer cash, so carry a little
Entry (US/UK/AU) | US passports: no visa for 90 days or less. See the checklist below for the rest
From the airport | Trolleybus 59 to Nádraží Veleslavín, then metro line A to the centre. Or the Airport Express to the main station, 200 CZK
Daily budget | About 1,030 CZK (budget) or 2,080 CZK (mid-range) per person, hotel and Castle ticket not included
:::

## What's New in 2026

- **New fares from 1 January 2026.** A 90-minute ticket is 46 CZK in the PID Lítačka app and 50 CZK on paper. The 72-hour pass is 340 CZK in the app and 350 CZK on paper (PID, checked 31 Oct 2026).
- **The EU Entry/Exit System (EES) is fully running.** It has been fully operational since 10 April 2026. As a non-EU visitor you register at the border, so allow extra time in the passport queue.
- **ETIAS is not here yet.** I could not find an official start date. Do not pay a website that sells an "ETIAS" for your December trip.
- **The markets open on Saturday 28 November 2026 and run to 6 January 2027.** The big ones are Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square, open 10:00 to 22:00 daily (Prague Experience, checked 31 Oct 2026).
- **Holiday hours are not settled.** Sources disagree on 24 to 26 December and 31 December, so this plan avoids those days.

### Afternoon

Walk through the lanes to the Powder Tower (Prašná brána, 화약탑) and look at the Municipal House (Obecní dům) from outside. Then follow Na Příkopě to Wenceslas Square (바츨라프 광장), the second main market.

::: Food | Market food in Old Town and Wenceslas Square
Style | Sausage (klobása), potato pancake (bramborák), trdelník (sweet rolled pastry) and mulled wine (svařák)
Price | About 100 to 250 CZK per snack (planning estimate, not an official price)
Tip | Trdelník is mostly a tourist snack and not an old Czech dish. Try the sausage or the potato pancake if you want a local taste
:::

::: Place | Wenceslas Square Christmas Market (Václavské náměstí)
Address/Nearest station | Wenceslas Square. Metro: Muzeum (lines A and C) or Můstek

Must / Nice / Skip | Nice

### Evening

Go back to Old Town Square. The tree light show runs every day at 16:30, 17:30, 18:30, 19:30, 20:30 and 21:30 (Prague Experience). Pick one slot, stand near the tree, and then eat dinner away from the square, where prices are usually lower.

## Which Ticket? The Maths

PID tickets are time based. A 90-minute ticket lets you change between metro, tram and bus until the time runs out. This plan uses five separate rides:

| Ride | Where | Ticket |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Airport to your hotel (trolleybus 59 + metro A) | 90 min, 46 CZK |
| 2 | Day 2, metro A to Malostranská | 90 min, 46 CZK |
| 3 | Day 3, metro C to Vyšehrad | 90 min, 46 CZK |
| 4 | Day 3, Vyšehrad area to Náměstí Míru | 90 min, 46 CZK |
| 5 | Hotel to the airport | 90 min, 46 CZK |

| Option | Price (app) | For this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Five 90-minute tickets | 230 CZK (~$10.5) | Best |
| Three 24-hour tickets | 420 CZK (~$19.3) | 190 CZK too much |
| One 72-hour ticket | 340 CZK (~$15.6) | 110 CZK too much |

The 72-hour ticket wins only with eight or more rides (340 / 46 = 7.4). If you add day trips, run the numbers again. Paper tickets cost 50 CZK each, 4 CZK more than the app. Prices are for ages 15 to 60. All figures are from PID's tariff valid from 1 Jan 2026 (checked 31 Oct 2026). I did not check the zone coverage of the airport ticket, so confirm at the stop.

## Getting There & Around

::: Route | Prague Airport (PRG) → City centre
Option 1 | Trolleybus 59 to Nádraží Veleslavín (about 15 minutes), then metro line A to the centre (about 15 minutes). One 90-minute ticket covers both: 46 CZK app, 50 CZK paper (PID)
Option 2 | Airport Express to the main station (Praha hlavní nádraží), 200 CZK one way (PID)
Late night | Night buses run when the metro is closed. Routes not checked
Tip | The old bus 119 became trolleybus 59 in March 2024 (a guide). Older blog posts still say 119
:::

The old centre is compact. Most of this plan is on foot, and the metro helps for the Castle and Vyšehrad. Use a taxi app or the official taxi rank at the airport, because street taxis can overcharge.

## FAQ

### Is the Prague Christmas market free?
Yes, walking into the Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square markets is free. You pay for food, drink and gifts.

### When do the Prague Christmas markets start and end in 2026?
The main markets open on 28 Nov 2026 and run to 6 Jan 2027, from 10:00 to 22:00 daily (Prague Experience).

### Do I need the 72-hour transport pass?
Not for this plan. Five rides cost 230 CZK in the app, while the 72-hour pass costs 340 CZK. It pays off only with eight or more rides.

### Can I pay by card at the markets?
Many stalls may take cards, but I did not confirm this. Carry a little cash in koruna to be safe.
