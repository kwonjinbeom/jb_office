## What's New in 2026

- **Australians no longer need a visa.** Reports say a presidential decision published on 17 April 2026 allows Australian ordinary-passport holders to visit for tourism for up to 90 days in any 180 days. I could not read the official text, so check Smartraveller or the Turkish embassy before you fly.
- **British visitors:** visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180 days. Your passport must be valid for at least 150 days after you arrive and have one blank page (GOV.UK, checked 29 Oct 2026).
- **US visitors:** most guides say you still need an e-Visa, for up to 90 days. The fee in these guides ranges from US$20 to US$60, so I will not give one number. Apply only on the official site, evisa.gov.tr.
- **The Basilica Cistern changed hands in June 2026.** It moved to a state agency, closed for a short time and reopened on 6 June. Prices and evening sessions may still be changing (secondary source).
- **Cards only at the cistern.** Reports say it takes bank cards and İstanbulkart, not cash.

## Is the Museum Pass Worth It?

The Museum Pass Istanbul costs about 6,500 TL and lasts 5 days from your first scan (secondary source, April 2026). It covers Topkapı, the Archaeology Museums, Chora and the Hagia Sophia history museum. It does not cover the Hagia Sophia main floor, the Basilica Cistern or Galata Tower.

| Item | Single ticket (approx. TL) |
|---|---|
| Topkapı Palace plus Harem | 2,500 |
| Archaeology Museums | 650 |
| Chora (history section) | 770 |
| Hagia Sophia History and Experience Museum | 960 |
| All four bought one by one | 4,700 to 5,100 |
| Museum Pass | 6,500 |

On this plan you enter two covered places, so single tickets cost about 3,150 TL, less than half the pass. Even if you visit all four, the single tickets add up to about 4,700 to 5,100 TL, which is still below 6,500 TL. Skip the pass unless you plan more covered museums than these. Prices come from one secondary source and may differ on muze.gov.tr.

## Getting There & Around

::: Route | Istanbul Airport → Sultanahmet
Metro | M11 to Gayrettepe, M2 to Vezneciler or Yenikapı side, then tram T1. About 60 to 80 minutes (secondary source)
Bus | Havaist airport bus to Aksaray, then tram T1. About 60 to 105 minutes (secondary source)
Night | M11 does not run from midnight to 6:00. Use a taxi or a pre-booked transfer if you land late
:::

Buy an İstanbulkart at kiosks or shops near the stations, or use a mobile version. Reports in 2026 put one full ride at about 42 TL after a February rise, and the card at about 165 TL. Fares drop for transfers within two hours, but the exact steps differ by source. Check the İBB or İstanbulkart official page.

Tram T1 links Sultanahmet, Gülhane, Eminönü, Karaköy and the Grand Bazaar area. It is the line you will use most. The streets are cobbled and hilly, so wear shoes with grip.

## FAQ

### Is the Museum Pass worth it for three days?

Not for this plan. Single tickets for Topkapı and the Archaeology Museums cost about 3,150 TL, against 6,500 TL for the pass.

### Is Istanbul safe to visit right now?

Smartraveller kept its advice at "exercise a high degree of caution" in April 2026 because of wider Middle East tensions. Check your government's advice just before you fly.

### Can I visit mosques at any time?

No. Visitors cannot enter during prayer times. Plan mosque visits between prayers, and dress modestly.

### Do I need cash?

Mostly cards work, and the Basilica Cistern takes cards only. Keep a little cash for tea, small shops and the bazaar.
