# 3 Days in Barcelona (Dec 2026): Sagrada Família Timed Tickets and the Hola Barcelona Card Maths

> This plan is for first-time visitors from the US, UK and Australia who want three days in Barcelona in December. Day 1 is Sagrada Família and the Eixample, Day 2 is the Gothic Quarter and El Born, and Day 3 is Park Güell and Montjuïc. Fares and hours checked on 28 Oct 2026, and anything I could not confirm on an official page is marked.

## What's New in 2026

- **EES is now in use.** The EU Entry/Exit System registers non-EU visitors on their first entry. You may be asked to give fingerprints and a photo at a booth. It is free, and your record lasts 3 years (GOV.UK, checked 28 Oct 2026). Allow extra time at the border on arrival and when you leave.
- **ETIAS is not required yet.** The EU says ETIAS was not in operation when I looked, and US and UK visitors pay nothing for it today. The fee is planned at €20. Start dates in news reports differ ("late 2026" in older pages, 2027 in newer ones), so check travel-europe.europa.eu before you fly.
- **Transport fares rose on 15 January 2026.** A single metro or bus ticket is now €2.90 and a 10-ride T-casual is €13.00 (TMB and press reports).
- **Sagrada Família sells timed tickets.** Your ticket is only valid for the slot you pick, and late arrivals cannot enter. Under-11s are free (official price page).
- **Holiday hours are short.** On 25 and 26 December and on 1 and 6 January, Sagrada Família opens only 9:00 to 14:00.

Book the first slot of the day. The nave is quietest then, and the morning light is good. Buy only on sagradafamilia.org, because the price list there is the real one. Resellers often charge much more for the same entry.

[Open Sagrada Família in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sagrada+Familia+Barcelona)

Entry rules to know before you go:

- Your ticket is valid for your time slot only. Late arrivals cannot enter.
- Bags and rucksacks are checked. Towers do not allow bags, and there are no lockers for suitcases, so leave luggage at your hotel.
- Food and drink are not allowed inside.
- Dress code: no see-through clothes or swimwear, and trousers and skirts must reach at least mid-thigh.

## Is the Hola Barcelona Card Worth It?

The Hola Barcelona Travel Card gives unlimited rides for 48, 72, 96 or 120 hours from your first use. It covers metro, bus, NitBus, the Montjuïc funicular, trams, FGC urban trains (zone 1) and Rodalies (zone 1), plus one airport metro trip each way. The Montjuïc cable car is not covered (holabarcelona.com, checked 28 Oct 2026).

The official page only shows a "from" price, so I used a price from guides: about €27.30 for 72 hours. Guides disagree, from €27.30 up to €30, so check the live price.

Fares used (TMB and 2026 press reports): single €2.90, T-casual 10 rides €13.00, airport ticket €5.90 each way, T-dia (24 hours) €12.00.

| Option for 3 days (about 12 rides) | Cost |
|---|---|
| Hola Barcelona 72 h (airport round trip included) | about €27.30 (guide price, unconfirmed) |
| T-casual (10 rides) + 2 single tickets + 2 airport tickets | €13.00 + €5.80 + €11.80 = €30.60 |
| T-dia x 3 + 2 airport tickets | €36.00 + €11.80 = €47.80 |
| If you ride 10 times or fewer: T-casual + 2 airport tickets | €13.00 + €11.80 = €24.80 |

This plan has about 12 rides: Day 1 about 4, Day 2 about 3, Day 3 about 5. At that count the card saves about €3.30. If you plan to walk more and ride 10 times or fewer, T-casual is cheaper. Do not buy day passes for a short stay like this.

One tip: the 72 hours run from your first scan, not by calendar day. Use it first when you leave the airport, and plan your last ride before the clock ends.

## FAQ

### Do I need to book Sagrada Família in advance?

Yes. Tickets are for a fixed time slot, and late arrivals cannot enter. Book on sagradafamilia.org as soon as you know your dates.

### Is the Hola Barcelona card worth it for three days?

With about 12 rides it saves around €3.30 against T-casual plus airport tickets, using a guide price for the card. With 10 rides or fewer, T-casual is cheaper.

### Do I need ETIAS for Barcelona in December 2026?

Not yet. When I checked, the EU said ETIAS was not in operation. Check travel-europe.europa.eu before you fly in case a start date is announced.

### What time does Sagrada Família close in December?

Monday to Saturday it opens 9:00 to 18:00, and Sunday 10:30 to 18:00. On 25 and 26 December it opens 9:00 to 14:00.

### Is the Christmas market open when I visit?

The Santa Llúcia fair ran from 28 November to 23 December in 2025. I could not confirm the 2026 dates, so check the official site before you plan around it.
