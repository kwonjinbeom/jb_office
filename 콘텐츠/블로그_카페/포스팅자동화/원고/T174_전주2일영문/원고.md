## 손질하면 좋아지는 곳 (사용자 몫)

1. `[사진]` 자리에 실제 전주 가을 사진을 넣어요 (한국관광콘텐츠랩 공공누리 1유형 또는 위키미디어, 캡션에 출처). 못 구하면 그 줄을 지워요.

**리드(첫 인용)** — 10월 28일 전주 공식 영문 페이지와 시간표 기준. 서울(용산역)에서 KTX로 2시간이 안 걸린다. 1일차 한옥마을·경기전·전동성당·야경, 2일차 풍남문·남부시장·전주향교. 서울~전주 일반실 34,600원, 영어 글에서 보기 힘든 시간표와 예산표를 넣었다.

**위치 지도 자리** — 전북 전주 위치 지도.

**Jeonju at a Glance (전주 한눈에)** — Info 카드: 위치(전북, 서울 남쪽), 좋은 때(11월 초중순 단풍), 필요한 날 2일, 서울에서 KTX 약 1시간 46분~1시간 55분 / 고속버스 약 2시간 40분, 결제, 입국(K-ETA 2026-12-31까지 면제), 한글 검색어.

**Before You Go (2026 바뀐 것)** — ① 9월 1일 KTX·SRT 통합, 코레일+ 앱, 운임 평균 10% 인하, 전라선 증편. ② 10월 1일부터 기차표 2개월 전 예매. ③ 6월 4일부터 네이버 지도 여권 인증. ④ K-ETA 면제 12월 31일 끝, 2027 발표 없음. ⑤ 한옥마을 연희 행진은 10월 31일에 끝나 11월엔 없음.

**Admission & Hours, Checked on 28 Oct 2026 (입장료·시간 대조표)** — 경기전 3,000원(외지인 어른)·문화가 있는 날 11월 25일 무료, 전동성당 무료 09:00–17:00, 전주향교 무료 10:00–18:00. 경기전 시간이 출처마다 다름(10:00 vs 09:00 시작).

# 2 Days in Jeonju (Nov 2026): KTX from Seoul, Hanok Village, Bibimbap and a Timed Plan

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> Fares and hours checked on 28 Oct 2026 against Jeonju's official Hanok Village pages and the current KTX timetable. Take the KTX from Seoul's Yongsan Station (under 2 hours, ₩34,600 standard class on a sample day), spend Day 1 in Hanok Village and Gyeonggijeon Shrine, and Day 2 at Pungnammun Gate, Nambu Market and the old Confucian school. The weekend "Yeonhui" parade in the village ended on 31 Oct 2026, so there is no parade in November.

Jeonju (전주) is the food capital of Korea and the home of bibimbap. Its old streets of tiled-roof houses (hanok, 한옥) sit close together, so two days on foot and by city bus is enough.

This plan is for first-time visitors who take the train. Where we could not confirm a number, we say so.

## Before You Go (2026 Changes)

- **KTX and SRT merged on 1 Sep 2026.** Booking moved to the Korail+ app (코레일+). KTX fares fell about 10% on average, and Jeolla Line trains were added (weekday 40 to 45 a day, weekend 45 to 54).
- **Train tickets open 2 months ahead** (since 1 Oct 2026). Weekends in November fill up early.
- **Naver Map passport verification** (since 4 June 2026): you can book and pay in Naver Map without a Korean phone number.
- **K-ETA exemption ends 31 Dec 2026.** No 2027 extension was announced as of 28 Oct 2026.
- **The Hanok Village Yeonhui parade ended on 31 Oct 2026.** It ran on Saturdays at 15:00 from 18 Apr. Do not plan around it in November.

## Getting Around

**The old town is walkable.** Hanok Village, Gyeonggijeon Shrine, Jeondong Cathedral, Pungnammun Gate and Nambu Market are all within about 1.5 km of each other. You only need a bus to and from the station.

- **Bus 79** appears on both of the official Jeonju pages for the station route. Bus 508, 536, 542, 546 and 119 are also on both lists. The two official pages disagree on other numbers, so open Naver Map and check the live arrival time.
- From the **Express Bus Terminal**, bus 79 (and 999, 1994) goes to the village in about 15 minutes. A taxi costs about ₩5,000–6,000.
- Get off in front of **Jeondong Catholic Church (전동성당)**, the usual village stop.
- By car, the Daesung lot is free and a shuttle runs 10:00–19:00. The public lot at the village costs money, so go by train if you can.
- The village tourist information office (Girin-daero 99, 기린대로 99) is open 24 hours: +82-63-282-1330.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Transit card.** A Korean transit card such as T-money makes bus rides easier and cheaper. Keep a little cash too.
- **Foreign cards.** Most restaurants take Visa and Mastercard. Market stalls often want cash, so carry ₩30,000–50,000.
- **Naver Map.** Better than Google Maps for Korean buses and walking. Since 4 June 2026 you can verify with your passport and book or pay without a Korean number.
- **Kakao T and Papago.** Kakao T for taxis, Papago for translating menus.
- **Tax refund.** Look for "Tax Free" shops. From 2024, an instant refund works up to under ₩1,000,000 per purchase and ₩5,000,000 per trip. Ask at the shop, as rules can change.
- **1330 travel hotline.** 24-hour help in English, Japanese and Chinese. Dial 1330 in Korea or +82-2-1330 from abroad.
- **Village tourist office.** Open 24 hours at Girin-daero 99 for maps and bag questions: +82-63-282-1330.

## FAQ

**Can I visit Jeonju as a day trip from Seoul?**
Yes. The KTX takes under 2 hours each way. But the village is best in the evening, so one night is more relaxed.

**How much is Gyeonggijeon Shrine?**
₩3,000 for an adult from outside Jeonju (checked 28 Oct 2026). It is free on the last Wednesday of each month, which is 25 Nov 2026.

**Do I need a K-ETA to visit Korea in 2027?**
The exemption for 22 countries and regions ends on 31 Dec 2026 (KST). As of 28 Oct 2026, no 2027 extension was announced. Check k-eta.go.kr before you book.

**When do the leaves peak?**
The national 2026 foliage map expects the Jeolla region to peak in early to mid November. We could not find a forecast for Jeonju alone.

**Is the Saturday parade in Hanok Village on in November?**
No. The Yeonhui parade ran from 18 April to 31 October 2026.

The best single tip: book your KTX seats the day the 2-month window opens, and keep the evening of Day 1 free for the village streets.
