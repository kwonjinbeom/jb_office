# 2 Days in Daegu (Nov 2026): KTX from Seoul, Seomun Night Market, Apsan Cable Car and the Suseong Biennale

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> Fares and hours checked on 31 Oct 2026: the fastest KTX from Seoul Station to Dongdaegu takes 1 hour 38 minutes (₩43,100 standard class), and the Suseong Biennale at Suseongmot Lake is free until 22 Nov. Spend Day 1 on Apsan Mountain (앞산) and at the Seomun Night Market (서문야시장), which opens only from Friday to Sunday. Day 2 is the biennale and the train home. We could not open the cable car's official site, so we mark its fare "reported".

Daegu (대구) is a large inland city in the southeast, about two hours from Seoul by high-speed train. It has a hill with a cable car, one of Korea's best-known traditional markets and, until 22 Nov, an outdoor design show by a lake.

This plan is for first-time visitors who travel by train and have no car. Where we could not confirm a number, we say so.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Book the KTX early.** Use the Korail English site or app, or a station kiosk. Friday evening and Saturday trains fill first.
- **Transit card.** A Korean transit card such as T-money works on Daegu city buses and the metro. Keep cash for night market stalls.
- **Foreign cards.** The cable car and most restaurants take Visa and Mastercard. Carry ₩30,000–50,000 in cash for small stalls.
- **Naver Map.** Better than Google Maps for Korean buses and live arrival times. Since 4 June 2026 you can verify with your passport and book or pay without a Korean number.
- **Kakao T and Papago.** Kakao T for taxis, Papago for translating menus.
- **Tax refund.** Look for "Tax Free" shops. From 2024, an instant refund works up to under ₩1,000,000 per purchase and ₩5,000,000 per trip. Ask at the shop, as rules can change.
- **1330 travel hotline.** 24-hour help in English, Japanese and Chinese. Dial 1330 in Korea or +82-2-1330 from abroad.
- **Cable car office.** Ask about wind, closures and the last ride: +82-53-656-2994.

## FAQ

### Can I visit Daegu as a day trip from Seoul?
Yes, because the KTX takes about two hours each way. But the Seomun Night Market only opens Friday to Sunday and the cable car is best near sunset, so one night is much more comfortable.

### Is the Seomun Night Market open every day?
No. In 2026 it opens only Friday, Saturday and Sunday, from March to December. Friday and Saturday hours are 19:00–23:30, and Sunday is 19:00–22:30. Weather or holidays can change this, so check the market's notices.

### Do I have to pay for the Suseong Biennale?
The data page we used lists it as free. Some activities need an advance sign-up. Check the hours too, because the page lists them under "performance hours".

### Do I need a K-ETA to visit Korea in 2027?
The exemption for 22 countries and regions ends on 31 Dec 2026 (KST). We did not find an official 2027 extension. Check k-eta.go.kr before you book.

### What is the Apsan Cable Car fare?
We found ₩14,000 round trip for adults on a Korea Tourism data page, and ₩10,500 on older English guides. The official site did not open for us, so call +82-53-656-2994 or check the homepage before you go.

The best single tip: book the Friday morning KTX, ride the cable car in daylight, and save your appetite for the night market.
