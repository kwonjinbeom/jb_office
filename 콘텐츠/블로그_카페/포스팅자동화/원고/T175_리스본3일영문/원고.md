# 3 Days in Lisbon (Dec 2026): Hills, Belém and a Sintra Day Trip

<!-- 제휴가 있으면 이 줄에 대가성 문구 -->

> This plan is for first-time visitors from the US, UK and Australia who want three days in Lisbon in December. Day 1 covers the old hills and Tram 28, Day 2 is Belém, and Day 3 is a day trip to Sintra. Fares and hours checked on 28 Oct 2026.

## Trip at a Glance

::: Info | Lisbon in 3 days
🗓 Best for | First visit, walkers who do not mind hills, history and view lovers
🌡 Weather | Mild but often wet in December. The sun sets early, so start each day on time
💱 Currency & cards | Euro (€). €1 is about US$1.12 (ECB, 27 Oct 2026). Cards work almost everywhere
🛂 Entry (US/UK/AU) | No visa for short stays. See the checklist below
✈️ From the airport | Red Line metro from Aeroporto station into the city. Single ticket €1.90 plus a €0.50 card (secondary source)
💸 Daily budget | About €37 to €55 (budget) or €67 to €100 (mid-range) per person, hotel not included

## What's New in 2026

- **EES is live.** The EU Entry/Exit System has run at all Schengen border crossing points since 10 April 2026. On your first entry you may give fingerprints and a photo at a booth. It is free. Plan for a longer queue, and for extra time when you leave. (GOV.UK, checked 28 Oct 2026)
- **ETIAS is not here yet.** The EU timetable points to the last quarter of 2026, and the exact date is announced months before. I could not find a start date on an official page, so do not pay any site that sells "ETIAS" now. Use travel-europe.europa.eu only.
- **Tourist tax.** Lisbon charges a city tax per person per night at the hotel. Reports say €4 a night for up to 7 nights, but I could not confirm this on an official page. Ask your hotel.
- **Tram 28 costs more on board.** Reports say €3.30 if you pay on the tram, against €1.90 with a rechargeable card. Check Carris before you go.

## Day 1 · Alfama and Baixa (Alfama 알파마)

| Time | Stop | Time there | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Tram 28 from Martim Moniz | 30 min | Ride up to Graça |
| 10:00 | Miradouro da Senhora do Monte (viewpoint) | 30 min | Walk or tram |
| 11:00 | Alfama old streets and Santa Luzia viewpoint | 1.5 h | Walk downhill |

| 14:30 | Praça do Comércio and Rua Augusta | 1 h | Walk |
| 16:30 | Free time, then dinner | - | - |

### Morning

Start early. Tram 28 is the most famous line in town, and the cars fill up fast later in the day. Board at the first stop, Martim Moniz (마르팅 모니즈), to get a seat. The ride climbs through narrow streets, so hold on.

::: Route | Tram 28
Fare | €3.30 if you pay on board. €1.90 with a Navegante rechargeable card (secondary source, check Carris)
Day pass | 24-hour Carris/Metro pass €7.25. It pays for itself with four rides
Tip | Keep your bag in front of you. Crowded trams attract pickpockets
:::

[Open Tram 28 start in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Martim+Moniz+Lisbon)

[사진] Tram 28 on a narrow street in Alfama (Wikimedia Commons, credit required)

The best views are the free ones. Get off at the Graça stop and walk to the viewpoint, then wander down through Alfama (알파마), the oldest district. The lanes are steep and the cobbles are slippery when wet, so wear shoes with grip.

[Open Miradouro da Senhora do Monte in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Miradouro+da+Senhora+do+Monte+Lisbon)

### Afternoon

Walk down to Baixa (바이샤), the flat grid of streets rebuilt after the 1755 earthquake. It ends at the big riverside square, Praça do Comércio (프라사 두 코메르시우). The square is free, and it is a good place to watch the evening light.

::: Food | Lunch in Baixa
Style | Small restaurants with a daily set menu (prato do dia)
Price | About €10 to €16 per person (planning estimate, not an official price)
Tip | Skip places with a person outside calling you in
:::

### Afternoon

Walk to the Monument to the Discoveries (Padrão dos Descobrimentos) and sit by the river. Belém is also known for custard tarts (pastéis de nata). Expect a queue at the most famous shop, and know that many cafés nearby sell them too. I did not verify opening hours or prices for the shops.

### Morning

Sintra is cooler and wetter than Lisbon. Bring a rain jacket. Take the train from Rossio station to Sintra. The fare is reported at about €2.30 each way (secondary source). Then take a bus or taxi uphill, because the walk to Pena is long and steep.

::: Place | Pena Palace (Palácio Nacional da Pena)
📍 Address/Nearest station | Estrada da Pena, Sintra. Bus from Sintra station
🕘 Hours | Palace 9:30 to 18:30, park 9:00 to 19:00. The site lists last entry times, so check them on the day
🎟 Admission (checked) | Essential visit €20. Park only €12. Complete visit €45 (adults 18 to 64)
🚌 Getting there | Bus from Sintra station or a taxi
⏱ Time needed | 3 hours
✅ Must / Nice / Skip | Must. Book a palace time slot online
:::

Palace tickets are for a fixed date and time. Book on the official Parques de Sintra site only. Fog can hide the palace in winter, so keep your plan flexible.

## FAQ

**Is Tram 28 worth it in December?**
Yes, if you go early and treat it as a short ride to the hills. Do not use it to go from A to B at noon.

**Are Belém sites open on Mondays?**
No. The Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower are closed on Mondays, 25 Dec and 1 Jan. Put Belém on a Tuesday to Sunday.

**Can I do Sintra without booking?**
Pena Palace tickets are for a fixed date and time, so book ahead. The park and the Moorish Castle are easier to visit on the day.

**Do I need a car?**
No. Train, metro, trams and buses cover this plan.
