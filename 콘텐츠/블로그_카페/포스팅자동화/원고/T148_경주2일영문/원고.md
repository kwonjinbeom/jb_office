## Getting Around

**Downtown is walkable.** Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Gyochon Village, Woljeonggyo Bridge and Donggung Palace sit in one flat area of about 2 km. You do not need a bus for Day 1 once you are in town.

**Buses 10 and 11 are the "golden loop."** They run the same circle in opposite directions: bus terminal → downtown → Bomun Lake → Bulguksa → Gyeongju National Museum → Donggung Palace → terminal. First bus 06:00, last bus 21:40 from the terminal, about every 20 minutes.

- To Bulguksa fast: **bus 11** (via Donggung Palace and the museum).
- From Bulguksa back to the museum: **bus 10**.
- Seokguram: **bus 12** runs only between Bulguksa and Seokguram. Waits can be long in peak season.
- From Gyeongju Station: **bus 70** (also 50, 51 and others) to the terminal area; **express bus 1150** goes to Bomun Lake and Bulguksa.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Transit card.** A Korean transit card such as T-money saves ₩50 per bus ride and makes transfers easier. Top it up at convenience stores. Keep some cash, because bus cash fares need coins and small notes.
- **Foreign cards.** Most restaurants and shops take Visa and Mastercard. Some apps and very small shops may not accept foreign cards, so carry ₩30,000–50,000 in cash.
- **Naver Map.** Better than Google Maps for buses and walking in Korea. Since 4 June 2026 you can verify with your passport and book or pay without a Korean number.
- **Kakao T and Papago.** Kakao T for taxis, Papago for translating menus and signs.
- **Tax refund.** Look for "Tax Free" shops. From 2024, purchases from ₩15,000 qualify, and an instant refund at the shop works up to under ₩1,000,000 per purchase and ₩5,000,000 per trip. Ask at the shop, as rules can change.
- **1330 travel hotline.** 24-hour help in English, Japanese and Chinese. Dial 1330 in Korea or +82-2-1330 from abroad.
- **Night view timing.** Donggung Palace stops selling tickets at 21:30. Arrive by about 20:30 on weekends.

## FAQ

**Is Bulguksa Temple free in 2026?**
Yes. Bulguksa and Seokguram have been free since 4 May 2023 (checked on both temples' official sites on 26 Oct 2026). Parking costs ₩2,000.

**Can I do Gyeongju as a day trip from Busan?**
Yes. The KTX takes about 30 minutes. But if you want Donggung Palace at night and both temples, stay one night.

**Do I need a K-ETA to visit Korea in 2027?**
The exemption for 22 countries and regions (including the US, UK, Australia and Japan) ends on 31 Dec 2026 (KST). As of 26 Oct 2026, no 2027 extension was announced. Check k-eta.go.kr before you book.

**When do the leaves peak in Gyeongju?**
The 2026 national foliage map (Korea Tourism Organization and Korea National Arboretum) expects the Gyeongsang region to peak in early to mid November.

**Is Singyeongju Station the same as Gyeongju Station?**
Yes. Singyeongju KTX station was renamed Gyeongju Station on 28 Dec 2023. The old downtown Gyeongju Station closed in 2021.

The best single tip: book your KTX seat as soon as the 2-month window opens, then plan Day 1 around Donggung Palace after dark.
